# Google Ads – Two New Ad Groups (Gutter Cleaning + Commercial Pressure Washing)

**Account/Business:** Ray's Property Wash (Pressure Washing Xperts)
**Campaign:** Lead Campaign — Exact Match — Rays-pressure-washer
**Existing ad groups:** Ad group 1 (broad "power/pressure washing near me", currently Average) and Ad group 2 (exterior cleaning / window washing / pressure washing services, currently Excellent)
**New ad groups in this doc:** Ad group 3 (Gutter Cleaning) and Ad group 4 (Commercial Pressure Washing)
**Geo focus:** Lexington, SC only, per current advertising scope
**Ad Strength goal:** Excellent on both new ad groups

## Why these two themes

Ad group 1 already casts a wide net across house washing, driveways, decks,
fences, and windows. Ad group 2 owns the "exterior cleaning / window washing"
brand angle. Gutter cleaning and commercial pressure washing are real,
named services on the site that neither existing ad group targets with a
dedicated exact-match keyword list — so these two new groups add coverage
instead of competing with the existing ones for the same clicks. Every
headline and description below was checked against the two existing ad
groups' copy to confirm nothing is duplicated.

Both new ad groups' headlines lead with the literal popular keyword phrase
("Gutter Cleaning Lexington SC," "Commercial Pressure Washing") in multiple
headlines — this is the exact suggestion Google Ads is flagging as
outstanding on Ad group 1 ("Include popular keywords in your headlines"),
and it is what should push these two new groups to Excellent from the
start.

No em dashes anywhere in this copy. No pricing, "free quote," "instant
pricing," or estimate language — the site does not publish prices, so ads
must not imply it does. No mention of roof washing/treatment anywhere —
that is not a service this business sells.

## Notes for whoever runs this

- If Ad group 3 or 4 doesn't hit Excellent on the first save, the prompt
  above already tells Claude in Chrome to read Google's specific
  suggestion and adjust one or two lines rather than guessing — that
  matches how Ad group 2 got to Excellent and why Ad group 1 is stuck at
  Average (it's still missing "popular keywords in headlines," which both
  new groups lead with from headline #1).
- The existing account has "Business name field: empty (0/25)" and "no
  logo" noted on both current ad groups. That's an account-level asset,
  not something either new ad group can fix on its own, but it's worth
  flagging separately since it affects Ad Strength account-wide too.
- Sitelinks in Google Ads are often applied at the campaign level, so the
  existing 12 sitelinks may already show for these new ad groups without
  any action. The 4 new sitelinks per group above are additive, aimed
  specifically at gutter-intent and commercial-intent searches that the
  existing generic sitelink set doesn't address as directly.
